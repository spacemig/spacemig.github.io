About

I direct the Hawaiʻi Space Flight Laboratory at the University of Hawaiʻi at Mānoa and hold a research appointment at the Hawaiʻi Institute of Geophysics and Planetology. At HSFL we design, build, test and operate small satellites for space science.

I joined HSFL as an engineer in 2009. My Ph.D. work produced a multi-agent robotic system and guidance, navigation and control algorithms for distributed space missions. Since then I have led the lab's satellite testing facility, co-invented the COSMOS flight software framework, served as Deputy PI and systems engineer on NASA's HyTI mission, and taken on the role of PI for Neutron-2.

Software

COSMOS, the Comprehensive Open-architecture Solution for Mission Operations Systems, is an open-source flight software and toolchain for satellite operations and control. It runs HSFL's missions, and I co-invented it and lead its development.

Other code lives on my GitHub profile, including a Python satellite attitude simulator.
